Converted to Core ML with float16 embedding gathers and float32 encoder compute, masked mean pooling and L2 normalization.
The attention-mask sentinel is -10000 to avoid float16 overflow; measured parity is documented in multilingual-parity.json.

Tokenizer algorithms are ported to Swift from Hugging Face Tokenizers Unigram and
spm_precompiled's SentencePiece normalizer. These implementations are modified:
Swift value storage, frozen charsmap interpretation, no offsets/decoding/training.
https://github.com/huggingface/tokenizers/tree/v0.20.3
